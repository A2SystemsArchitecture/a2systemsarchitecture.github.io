A2 Pulse

Know Before It's Too Late

A2 Pulse is A2 Systems Architecture's real-time intervention intelligence system - the operational layer that converts diagnostic data into live, school-facing action. It connects diagnostic findings to intervention decisions, telling administrators and teachers which students need intervention, on which skills, at what dosage, before the window to act closes.

Teacher View - Classroom-level intelligence that tells teachers exactly which students need what, and when.

Built on prior diagnostic work - A2 Pulse operates on the architecture A2 Systems Architecture already built for your institution.

Institutional Learning Tool

A2 Watts

Guide Students to Answers. Never Give Them.

A2 Watts is a white-label Socratic learning guide built for K-12 institutions. Fully customized to your school's brand identity, it runs entirely in the browser with no backend infrastructure - making it FERPA-compliant by design and instantly accessible on any device. Every interaction guides students toward answers through questioning, never providing answers directly.

White-label and institution-branded - Your colors, mascot, avatar, and honor code language. Presents as your school's own tool.

FERPA-compliant by design - No student data collected. No backend infrastructure required. Runs entirely in the browser.

Academic integrity protected - Refuses to write essays, complete assignments, or produce submittable work. Every session ends with a more capable student.
