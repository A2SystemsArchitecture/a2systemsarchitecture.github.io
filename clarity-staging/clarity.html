WHAT IS A2 CLARITY

Understand the evidence. Empower the future.

A2 Clarity is A2 Systems Architecture's family-facing diagnostic service. It takes the assessment evidence your child already has — assessment scores, report cards, teacher feedback — and turns it into a clearer picture of what that evidence may actually be showing about your child's learning, written for parents and students, not just educators.

WHY IT MATTERS

Results can show what happened without explaining what it means.

Assessment results can show what happened without making it clear what the evidence may mean or what deserves attention next. A score or a grade is a data point, not a plan.

WHO IT HELPS

Parents and students seeking clarity.

Parents and students who want a clearer understanding of what existing assessment evidence may be showing about learning — before deciding what to do next.

One student. Many professional educators. One clearer learning picture.

A2 Clarity captures a meaningful picture of your child's learning at a specific point in time. It is designed to travel with your child, not define them permanently.

Clarity is a starting point, not a permanent identity.

WHAT A2 CLARITY IS — AND ISN'T

A clearer picture. Not a diagnosis, and not a placement decision.

A2 Clarity does not provide tutoring, therapy, or a medical or psychological diagnosis. It does not determine special-education eligibility or placement, and it does not guarantee agreement from any school or professional educator. It is a tool to help you understand evidence you already have and prepare for an informed conversation with the professionals in your child's life.

A2 Clarity provides educational diagnostic intelligence based on the assessment evidence provided. It is not a medical, psychological, or clinical diagnosis, a special-education eligibility determination, or a replacement for evaluation by a school or licensed professional.
